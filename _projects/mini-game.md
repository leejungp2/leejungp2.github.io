## Overview

Mini Game documents "I want to eat tteokbokki, but I want to die"
("떡볶이는 먹고 싶지만 죽고싶어"), a small game project from the Introduction to
Information, Culture, and Technology course. The game was planned to create an
indirect experience of depression through everyday actions and emotional
friction.

## Role

Design lead and planning support, 2021.

## Link

[Play the game](https://irenelynnq.github.io/isc20_tteokbokki/)

## Highlights

- Designed the visual direction and supported the game planning.
- Built the concept around the idea of indirectly experiencing depression.
- Designed stages around opening Instagram, getting out of bed, moving through a heavy body, and reaching the tteokbokki shop "정년다방."
- Prepared game screens, opening and closing flows, ending visuals, and a concept portfolio.

\section{Research Interests}
My research examines how people build and revise mental models of AI systems as
collaborators, as relational partners, and as tools in the workplace. I design and empirically evaluate LLM-based interactive systems to understand how
interaction design shapes these models, and how such systems can augment people at work and
support their well-being.

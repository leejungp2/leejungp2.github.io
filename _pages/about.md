I am an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in Intelligence and Information
Convergence at Seoul National University, advised by Professor Bongwon Suh at
the Human-Centered Computing Lab.

My research explores how people collaborate with AI systems, with a focus on
<span class="text-highlight-pink">cognitive psychology, human-AI relationships, and LLM-based tools</span>. These days, I am especially interested in how AI agents become part of everyday work and creative practices.

Before graduate school, I studied Psychology and Information Science and Culture
at Seoul National University.
